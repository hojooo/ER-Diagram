## Maintenance

`scripts/license-inventory.json` is the machine-readable source for this
table. Run `pnpm licenses:check` after adding, removing, or changing any direct
dependency. The check rejects unpinned versions, missing inventory or notice
entries, unknown or disallowed selected licenses, unresolved placeholders, and
`@dbml/connector` as a direct P0 dependency.
